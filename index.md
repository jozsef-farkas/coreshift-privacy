# CoreShift Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

## Overview

CoreShift is an abstract strategy game: a daily puzzle, games against the computer, Pass and Play, online matches, Cups and Challenges. We collect only what the game needs to work. There is no tracking and there are no ads. This policy explains what data is stored, where, who can see it, how long it is kept, and how you can have it deleted.

## Data stored on your device

- Game statistics (wins, losses, streaks, play time)
- Daily puzzle progress and streak
- Badge progress and tournament high scores
- App settings (appearance, sound, haptics, reminders, notification choices)

This data stays on your device. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset Statistics, or by deleting the app.

## Data shared through Apple services

**Game Center (optional).** If you sign in to Game Center, CoreShift shares leaderboard scores (for example your rating and your Challenges balance) and achievement progress with Apple, and online matches run through Game Center. Game Center is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

**Cups and Challenges (Apple iCloud public database).** If you take part in a Cup or a Challenge, CoreShift stores the following in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) **public database**:

- your Game Center player identifier and your display name (your Game Center name);
- the game data: the Cup or Challenge you host or join, your seat, the moves played, the result and its time;
- for Challenges, your rating at the time you post or accept.

**Other CoreShift players can see this data** (for example your display name and rating in the list of open Challenges, and the participants and results of a Cup). Choose a Game Center name you are comfortable showing to other players. CoreShift has no free-text chat. We do not receive your Apple ID, your real name or your email address from iCloud. This data is only stored if you use Cups or Challenges, and you need to be signed in to iCloud for them.

**Notifications.** If you allow notifications, reminders are scheduled on your device, and Challenge and Cup notifications are delivered by Apple's push service. We do not receive or store your device token.

## Purchases

CoreShift Pro is a one-time in-app purchase. Payment is processed entirely by Apple; we never see or store your payment details. We use RevenueCat (https://www.revenuecat.com) to verify and restore the purchase. RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier (it is generated for the app installation and is not linked to your name or email), your purchase history and the technical information its service needs, such as the app version. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

## What we do not do

- We do not track you, and we do not use your data to track you across other companies' apps or websites.
- We do not show advertisements.
- We do not use analytics or tracking SDKs.
- We do not sell your data.
- We do not collect your location, and we do not access your camera, microphone or contacts.

## How long data is kept

- **On your device:** until you delete the app or reset your statistics in the app.
- **Cups and Challenges (iCloud public database):** the records stay in the database until we delete them. They are not deleted automatically: finished Challenges, Cups, their moves and results, and the display name stored with them remain until we remove them or you ask us to. The app itself shows only recent finished Challenges (the last 7 days).
- **Purchase records (RevenueCat):** kept for as long as they are needed to restore your purchase, unless you ask for deletion (see below).
- **Game Center and iCloud settings:** kept by Apple, according to Apple's policies.

## Deleting your data

To have your Cup and Challenge data deleted, email **farkas_jozsef@icloud.com** with the subject "CoreShift data deletion" and tell us your Game Center display name. We may ask for a few details (for example approximate dates) to find your records. We delete the records stored with your player identifier and confirm by email, within 30 days. If you also want your purchase data removed from RevenueCat, say so in the same message and we will ask RevenueCat to delete it. Deleting a record also removes it for the other players who could see it, so a finished game may disappear from their history.

You can also stop sharing at any time: do not take part in Cups and Challenges, turn off Challenge notifications in Settings, or sign out of Game Center or iCloud on your device. Statistics on your device are deleted with Settings → Reset Statistics.

## Children's privacy

CoreShift is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users beyond what is described above, and there is no free-text chat between players.
