Privacy Policy
CoreShift
Last updated: April 5, 2026
Overview
CoreShift is a single-player and local multiplayer abstract strategy game. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.
Data We Collect
Data stored locally on your device:

Game statistics (wins, losses, streaks, play time)
Badge progress
Tournament high scores
App settings (theme, sound, haptic preferences)

This data never leaves your device. It is stored using Apple's standard local storage (SharedPreferences) and is not transmitted to any server.
Apple Game Center (optional):
If you sign in to Game Center, the following data is shared with Apple:

Leaderboard scores (win streak, total wins, tournament progress)
Achievement progress

Game Center data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect your name, email, or any personal information
We do not use analytics or tracking SDKs
We do not serve advertisements
We do not sell data to third parties
We do not collect location data
We do not access your camera, microphone, or contacts

In-App Purchases
CoreShift offers an optional Pro subscription. Payment is processed entirely by Apple. We do not store or access your payment information.
Children's Privacy
CoreShift is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children or any other users.
Data Retention
All locally stored data remains on your device until you delete the app or reset statistics manually within the app. You can delete all game data at any time via Settings → Reset Statistics.
Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected with an updated date at the top of this page.
